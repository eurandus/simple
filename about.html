Background

I studied Cognitive Sciences at UNIMAS, and that's where I became fascinated by how people process information, make decisions and learn. Ideas like cognitive load, mental models and attention still shape how I work: I'd rather design products that fit the way people naturally think than ask people to adapt to the limits of the technology.

I didn't go straight into product. I taught art for a year, then moved into systems: data pipelines at META Research and three years supporting ERP and supply-chain planning systems at Plexus Corp. In 2024 I moved into product management at Phillip Nova, and I'm now studying for an MBA at Universiti Sains Malaysia.

I think of agile as a mindset rather than a method. Every product is different, so if an approach doesn't work, I try something else. People who know me well describe me as upbeat, people-centred and driven by possibilities (ENFP, Enneagram 7).
