Regulation, legacy systems and tight deadlines aren't walls you hit at the end. Treat them as part of the design from day one, and they often make the product better.

In fintech this is concrete. In our [[trading-onboarding|onboarding rebuild]], AML and MAS requirements were built into the product design rather than checked afterwards.

Constraints also change over time, so the design has to be able to change with them — [[stay-like-water]].
