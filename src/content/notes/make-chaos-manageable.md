Real problems are messy, and pretending otherwise doesn't help.

What I can do is give the mess some structure, so people can see it, talk about it, and decide what to do next. My one-liner for this is: *I like to transform chaos into structured and controlled chaos.*

Structure starts with the people living in the chaos — [[people-first-then-the-system]] — and with being honest about the limits you're working within: [[constraints-are-design-material]].
