Good change moves you forward. Bad change shows you where forward isn't.

Either way, you know more than you did when you stood still. The only change I worry about is the one nobody learns from.

That's also why I don't treat a plan as finished — see [[stay-like-water]]. And when a change didn't work, it's often worth coming back to later: [[revisit-old-ideas]].
