MCP is very much a buzzword right now, and for me it's a fairly recent discovery — about six months old at the time of writing.

The idea isn't all that new. What has changed is that generative AI, and the machines we now run it on, can turn it into useful output in a fraction of the time it would have taken before.

So this Saturday morning went to learning more about MCP and exploring (or, honestly, brute-force testing) Manus AI. I'm looking forward to bringing MCP into my work — or at least tinkering with it properly.

I posted about it [on X](https://twitter.com/softserveotter/status/1943857924482510906) as part of learning in public.
