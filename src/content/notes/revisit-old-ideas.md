Most of my good ideas started as an older one I'd half forgotten — a note, a failed attempt, something I wasn't ready for yet.

Go back to where an idea came from. Look at what's changed since: the tools, the context, you. Then adapt it.

Ideas don't expire. They wait for the right moment.

This only works if the old ideas are somewhere you can find them, which is why I [[write-it-down]]. And it's the other half of [[every-change-teaches-you-something]]: a change that failed once might be exactly right now.
