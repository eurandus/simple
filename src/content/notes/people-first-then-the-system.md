Before deciding what to build, understand who it affects and how they actually work.

Products should fit the way people think, not the other way round. That idea comes straight from studying cognitive science — cognitive load, mental models, attention — and it still shapes how I work.

When we [[trading-onboarding|rebuilt our trading account onboarding]], I interviewed all four user groups and mapped their journeys before we decided anything. Four groups, four very different kinds of pain.
