This was my first project after moving from backend application engineering into product management. Sensitive and confidential details are left out.

## The problem

Opening a trading account ran through an outdated e-form system, and four groups of people felt it:

- **Credit Admin** — manual processes and data silos.
- **Regulatory teams** — compliance was hard to track.
- **Client Support** — little visibility into an application's status.
- **Sales** — a lengthy onboarding that hurt conversion.

The legacy system had a fragmented user experience, manual compliance checks, limited visibility across departments, and an outdated interface built on older HTML technology.

## How I approached it

**Discovery.** I interviewed all four user groups, mapped their current and desired journeys in Figma, identified the regulatory touchpoints and requirements, and analysed where the workflow was inefficient.

**Regulation as part of the design.** AML and MAS requirements were built into the product design rather than checked afterwards. I worked with our product designer to keep the flow compliant without compromising the experience, and to leave room for future regulatory updates — balancing regulatory rigour with operational efficiency.

**Alignment.** I set up an agile way of working across functions, ran regular stakeholder feedback sessions, and prioritised features by user impact and regulatory importance.

## What we built

A new platform, [accountopening.phillipnova.com.sg](https://accountopening.phillipnova.com.sg), replacing the old e-forms:

- **One experience** for every stakeholder group.
- **Automated compliance** — AML/MAS requirements built into the workflow.
- **Real-time visibility** — status tracking across departments.
- **A modern interface** — responsive, and aligned to the OneNova design system.

It was delivered through regular sprint reviews, with stakeholder feedback folded in continuously and a phased rollout to minimise disruption.

## Outcomes

- Onboarding time went from about 10 minutes to about 5.
- Less confusion for users, with improved satisfaction scores from UX analytics and forms.
- Better compliance efficiency for T+0 approvals.
- Smoother downstream work for Credit Admin, Operations, IT Support and other teams.

> When we are doing roadshows or showcasing our platform to the client, there is definitely a significant improvement in how we guide clients to open an account with us compared to the old platform.
>
> — Young Investor Program Lead, Phillip Nova

## Philosophy

> Agile is a mindset. It is not a tool nor methodology. It should shift like water.

As a product manager in fintech, I believe the best products integrate complex regulatory requirements while still delivering an intuitive, user-centred experience.
